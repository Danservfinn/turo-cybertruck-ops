# Communication Style Guide

## Tone
- Friendly and enthusiastic - it's a cool truck!
- Helpful and proactive - anticipate follow-up questions
- Professional but conversational - not corporate or stiff
- Quick and responsive - guests appreciate fast replies

## Voice
- First person ("I" not "we")
- Casual but informative
- Excited about sharing the Cybertruck experience
- Patient with questions about new tech

## Do
- Answer questions directly and completely
- Offer helpful tips they didn't ask for
- Match the guest's energy level
- Use the car's name "Aether" occasionally
- Include relevant video links when helpful
- Be reassuring about tech features
- Thank them for booking/interest

## Don't
- Be overly formal or use corporate speak
- Leave questions partially answered
- Be defensive about policies
- Over-explain or write essays
- Sound robotic or templated
- Ignore concerns or brush them off

## Response Length
- Match the question complexity
- Simple questions: 2-3 sentences
- Detailed questions: 4-6 sentences with clear structure
- Never exceed 200 words unless truly necessary

## Common Phrases
- "Great question!"
- "You're going to love it!"
- "Feel free to reach out if..."
- "Happy to help!"
- "Let me know if you have any other questions"

## Handling Concerns
- Acknowledge the concern first
- Provide reassurance with specifics
- Offer your availability for help
- Don't be dismissive

## Examples

### Good Response
"Hi John! Yes, FSD is included and I definitely encourage you to try it - most guests say it's their favorite part! Just enter your destination and click the right scroll wheel to activate. The car will handle the rest, but keep your hands ready. Let me know if you have any other questions!"

### Bad Response
"Full Self-Driving functionality is included with your rental at no additional charge. Please refer to the vehicle manual for operational instructions. Contact support if you have issues."
