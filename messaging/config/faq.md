# Frequently Asked Questions - Aether (Cybertruck)

## Access & Keys

### "How do I unlock/start the car?"
Download the Tesla app before pickup. You'll be added to Aether and can unlock, start, and control the car from your phone. There's also a backup wireless keycard in the center console. To lock, just walk away with your phone in your pocket!

### "Do I need a physical key?"
No! The Tesla app on your phone is your primary key. A wireless keycard is also available in the center console as backup.

---

## Driving

### "How do I shift gears? There's no gear shifter!"
Hold the brake and swipe up (Drive) or down (Reverse) on the touchscreen. To park, tap the P on screen or just unbuckle your seatbelt. Video: https://youtube.com/shorts/h6CgpHMuqC8

### "Where are the blinkers?"
There's no blinker stalk - look for the left and right blinker buttons on the steering wheel.

### "Is it hard to drive?"
Not at all! It drives like any automatic car. The steer-by-wire feels slightly different at first but you'll adjust within minutes. Most renters say it's surprisingly easy and fun to drive.

---

## Self-Driving (FSD)

### "How does Full Self-Driving work?"
FSD is included free! First, enter a destination in Navigation. Then click the right scroll wheel to activate. The car handles steering, acceleration, braking, and lane changes. Just keep your hands ready and pay attention.

### "Is FSD safe to use?"
Yes! We encourage it because it's statistically safer than human driving. But you must stay attentive - the car will prompt you to nudge the wheel occasionally.

### "What are 'strikeouts'?"
If you ignore the car's prompts to pay attention, you get a strikeout. 5 strikeouts = FSD disabled for a week. Just stay attentive and nudge the wheel when prompted.

### "How do I turn on FSD?"
First ensure it's enabled: https://youtu.be/Hk4LvfworVM
Then enter a destination and click the right scroll wheel.

## Charging

### "Where can I charge?"
Tesla Superchargers are everywhere and fastest (~35 min). The car's navigation will route you to chargers on road trips. You can also use other networks with the CCS adapter in the center console (~7 hours).

### "How much charge should I keep?"
Keep it between 20-80% for battery health. You can charge to 90% before a road trip. Return with at least 70%.

### "What if I don't return it charged?"
There's a $40 fee if returned below 70% charge.

## Pickup & Return

### "Can you deliver to RDU Airport?"
Yes! Delivery anywhere within 15 miles including RDU is $115.

### "What do I need to do before returning?"
1. Run through Zips Car Wash (Aether has a free membership!)
2. Vacuum the interior
3. Charge to 70%+
4. Make sure the charging adapter and keycard are in the center console

### "Where is Zips Car Wash?"
There's one near RDU airport and one in north Raleigh. The membership covers wash and vacuum for free!

### "What if I don't wash the car?"
$30 cleaning fee if not run through a car wash.

## Features

### "How do I use Car Wash Mode?"
Tap the Car icon > Service > Car Wash Mode. Video: https://youtube.com/shorts/tuLimcoDmQg

### "How do I adjust my seat/mirrors?"
Create your own driver profile or use the "Turo" profile. All adjustments are in car settings on the touchscreen. Video: https://youtu.be/80ohEGtk4k0

### "Can I use Auto-Park?"
Yes, and we recommend it! It's great for parallel parking and helps avoid curb damage to the rims.

## Road Trips

### "Can I take it on a road trip?"
Absolutely! The Supercharger network makes road trips easy. The car's navigation will plan charging stops for you. Just charge to 90% before you leave.

### "How many miles are included?"
200 miles per day. If you need more, let me know and we can discuss options.

## Concerns

### "What if I get a flat tire?"
Cybertruck has run-flat capable tires. Tesla Roadside Assistance is available, or call me and we'll figure it out together.

### "What if something goes wrong?"
Contact me anytime! I'm responsive and here to help make sure you have a great experience.

### "What about curb damage?"
Please be careful around curbs - rim damage will be invoiced. Use the auto-park feature for parallel parking!
