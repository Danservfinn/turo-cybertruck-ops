# Rental Policies - Aether (Cybertruck)

## Pickup & Delivery
- **Delivery available:** Up to 15 miles, including RDU Airport
- **Delivery fee:** $115
- **Vehicle delivered with:** 80%+ charge

## Mileage
- **Included:** 200 miles per day
- **Overage:** Charged per Turo's standard overage rates

## Charging Requirements
- **Return charge:** At least 70% (preferably 80%)
- **Skip charging fee:** $40 if returned below 70%
- **Battery range:** Keep between 20-80% during rental for battery health
- **Road trips:** Can charge up to 90% before long trips

## Cleaning Requirements
- **Expectation:** Run through car wash and vacuum before return
- **Free option:** Use Aether's Zips Car Wash membership (near RDU and north Raleigh)
- **Skip cleaning fee:** $30 if not cleaned

## Equipment & Fees
| Item | Location | Replacement Fee |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| CCS Charging Adapter | Center console | $50 |
| Wireless Keycard | Center console | $100 |

## Access & Keys
- Primary key: Tesla app (download for Apple/Android)
- Backup: Wireless keycard in center console
- Upon booking: You'll be added to Aether via Tesla app
- Locking: Walk away with phone - auto-locks

## Self-Driving & Auto-Park
- **FSD included:** Free to use, encouraged!
- **Auto-park:** Recommended for parallel parking
- **Safety note:** Pay attention to avoid "strikeouts"
- **5 strikeouts:** FSD disabled for one week

## Check-In
- Take photos and document car's condition for your benefit

## Check-Out Checklist
1. ☐ Run through Zips Car Wash (free with membership)
2. ☐ Vacuum interior
3. ☐ Return with 70%+ charge
4. ☐ CCS adapter in center console
5. ☐ Keycard in center console

## Damage Policy
- Rim damage from curbs will be invoiced
- Use auto-park for parallel parking to avoid curb rash
- Document any existing damage at check-in
