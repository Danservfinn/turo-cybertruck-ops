# Aether - 2024 Tesla Cybertruck Cyberbeast

## Quick Facts
- **Name:** Aether
- **Model:** 2024 Tesla Cybertruck Cyberbeast
- **Location:** Raleigh, NC
- **Features:** Full Self-Driving (FSD) included

## Specs
- Range: ~250-300 miles depending on driving style
- 0-60 mph: 2.6 seconds
- Bed length: 6ft with motorized tonneau cover
- GVWR: 6,843 lbs

## Features Included
- Full Self-Driving (FSD) - Supervised, free to use
- Auto-parking feature
- Premium audio system
- Camping mode
- Dog mode
- CCS charging adapter (in center console)
- Wireless keycard (in center console)

## Charging
- Tesla Superchargers: ~35 min to charge (fastest option)
- Third-party chargers with adapter: ~7 hours
- CCS adapter located in center console
- Must maintain battery between 20-80% for battery health
- Can charge to 90% before road trips

## Quirks & Tips
- No gear shifter - swipe up/down on screen while holding brake
- No blinker stalk - buttons on steering wheel
- Steer-by-wire feels different but you adjust quickly
- Frunk opens via app or touchscreen
- Tonneau cover is motorized
- Car wash mode: Car icon > Service > Car Wash Mode

## Self-Driving Notes
- Must input destination first, then click right scroll wheel
- Pay attention - car will "nag" you to nudge wheel
- 5 strikeouts = FSD disabled for one week
- Statistically safer than human drivers

## Zips Car Wash Membership
- Aether has a Zips membership - free car wash & vacuum
- Locations near RDU airport and north Raleigh
